# Metric definitions and reporting policy

| Metric | Definition | Sign convention |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Sum of credits posted to revenue accounts less debits | Positive |
| Expenses | Sum of debits posted to expense accounts less credits | Positive |
| Net profit | Revenue minus expenses | Positive is profit |
| Assets | Debit balances of asset accounts | Positive |
| Liabilities | Credit balances of liability accounts | Positive |
| Equity | Credit balances of equity accounts plus current-period net income in this demo | Positive |
| Expense variance | Budget minus actual expense | Positive is favourable |
| Expense variance % | (Budget - Actual) / Budget × 100 | Positive is favourable |
| Default rate | Defaults divided by loans with resolved outcomes | Higher means worse |
| Predicted default probability | Model-estimated probability of default | 0 to 1 |
| Risk band | Low <10%, Medium 10–30%, High >30% | Illustrative thresholds only |

## Controls
- Every journal ID must have equal total debits and credits.
- Assets must equal liabilities plus equity after period profit is included.
- Reconcile source totals before publication.
- Document reporting period, currency, accounting basis, and exclusions.
- Credit model outputs are decision-support demonstrations, not lending decisions.
